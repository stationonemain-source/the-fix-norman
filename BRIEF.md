## Their menu board (photo g13, read at full resolution)

Header "THE FIX / ENERGY & NUTRITION BAR", yellow and black. (Three name variants exist: the storefront
sign says ENERGY AND NUTRITION, the board says ENERGY & NUTRITION BAR, the cup sticker and Google say
ENERGY & NUTRITION LOUNGE. The site uses the Google/sticker form.)
- **The Power … Behind Liftoff …** — Vitamin C, B12, B6, Ginseng, Guarana Extract, Caffeine.
- **Prolessa Shot** — "curves appetite", "burns stubborn belly fat", "brings in good fats pushes out the bad".
- **Rebuild** — 24g Whey & Casein Protein · NSF Certified for Sport "all the way to Olympic Level" ·
  branched-chain amino acids · L-glutamine.
- **Additives** — Fiber $2.00 · Probiotic $1.00 · Prolessa Shot $4.00 · Beauty Booster $2.00 · **Cr7** $1.00 ·
  Pre Workout $4.00. (Re-read at full zoom 2026-09-16: an earlier pass misread Fiber as $1 and **Cr7 as
  "CrT" = creatine. Cr7 is a sports drink, not creatine** — "creatine" was on the page and has been removed.)
- "Check in and save up to $1" · "Ask how to get your combo FREE!!!!"

⚠️ **The prices are NOT on the site.** The board photo has no date on it and a 2024 review argues about a
$10.80 combo price, so the numbers may be stale. The page names the boosters and says the board and
today's prices are at the counter. Put prices back only once the owner confirms them.

## The drinks on the page

No public menu anywhere lists their flavour names — not Google, Yelp, Sirved, Zmenu, restaurantguru or
restaurantji, and Instagram cannot be harvested from this box. So the five hero drinks are **named for what
the photograph proves**, not for a menu item, and the board section says outright that flavours rotate and
you should point at a colour. The only drink named by name anywhere is the **cinnamon roll shake**, from
kat's review, and the page attributes it to the reviews rather than to the cup in the picture.
